## 🎙️ 1. Voice-First AI & Urdu Audio Logging
*Effort: Medium | Impact: Very High (Accessibility & Cultural Fit)*

### What it does:
Enables users who prefer speaking over typing to log entire meals and converse with the AI Coach in conversational Urdu, Punjabi, Roman Urdu, or English.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Natural Voice Meal Logging**:
  - *User speaks:* "Maine dopahar mein 2 chapatis, ek bowl daal mash aur thora sa kheere ka salad khaya hai."
  - Gemini / Whisper audio model extracts dishes, estimates portions, and automatically fills the macro database.
- **Gemini Live Conversational AI Coach**:
  - Bidirectional voice streaming for hands-free advice while cooking in the kitchen or walking.
- **Urdu Voice Responses**:
  - Coach replies with natural, localized Urdu speech for elderly users or non-English readers.

## 🩺 2. Biomarker Tracking & Lab Report OCR
*Effort: Medium | Impact: Very High (Clinical & Chronic Disease Management)*

### What it does:
Transforms NutriSense from a standard calorie counter into a clinical metabolic health management platform.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Lab Test PDF / Photo OCR Scanner**:
  - User snaps a photo of a diagnostic lab report (e.g. Chughtai Lab, Shaukat Khanum, Essa, IDC).
  - Gemini OCR extracts key biomarkers: **HbA1c, Fasting Blood Sugar, Lipid Profile (LDL/HDL/Triglycerides), Uric Acid, Creatinine, Vitamin D/B12**.
  - Automatically updates the user's clinical profile and flags dangerous trends.
- **Blood Glucose & Spike Tracker**:
  - Fasting and Post-prandial (2 hours post-meal) blood sugar logging.
  - Generates a **Meal-to-Spike Correlation Matrix** showing which specific meals caused blood sugar surges.
- **Blood Pressure & Sodium Tracker**:
  - Systolic/Diastolic BP logging with DASH diet recommendations and hypertensive alerts.

## 🍳 3. Desi Smart Recipe Generator ("Pantry-to-Plate")
*Effort: Low-Medium | Impact: High (Daily Retention)*

### What it does:
Helps users cook delicious, healthy Pakistani and South Asian meals using ingredients they already have in their kitchen.

### Key Capabilities:
- **"Fridg-e-Sense" (Visual Fridge/Pantry Scan)**:
  - User takes a picture of open fridge or pantry shelves.
  - AI identifies available ingredients (e.g. eggs, tomatoes, onions, spinach, chicken, daal) and proposes 3 healthy recipes fitted to remaining daily calories.
- **Healthier Desi Swaps**:
  - Suggests specific cooking tweaks: Air-fryer samosas, low-oil tarka, brown rice or cauliflower biryani, oats-thickened haleem, and zero-sugar kheer.
- **Budget-to-Protein Optimizer (PKR-focused)**:
  - Computes the most cost-effective protein sources available in local Pakistani bazaars (e.g. eggs, roasted chana, daal mash, chicken liver, curd/paneer).

## 💊 4. Medication Reminders & Drug-Food Interaction Guard
*Effort: Medium | Impact: High (Safety & Elderly Care)*

### What it does:
Ensures safe medication adherence and warns against dangerous food-drug cross-interactions.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Medication Scheduling**:
  - Schedule reminders for Metformin, Insulin, BP tablets, Statins, or vitamins.
  - Automatically reschedules medication times during Ramadan (Sehri vs. Iftar timing).
- **Drug-Food Interaction Engine**:
  - Warns if a logged food conflicts with active medications:
    - *e.g., Grapefruit juice with calcium channel blockers / BP meds.*
    - *e.g., High-potassium foods (bananas/spinach) with ACE inhibitors.*
    - *e.g., Chai/tea immediately after Iron supplements (tannins blocking absorption).*

## 🏃 5. Glucose-Blunting Workouts & Movement Coach
*Effort: Low-Medium | Impact: Medium-High (Metabolic Control)*

### What it does:
Pairs physical activity timing with meal intake to optimize insulin sensitivity and metabolic health.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Post-Meal "10-Minute Glucose Walk" Prompt**:
  - 20 minutes after logging a high-carb meal (e.g. Biryani/Naan), triggers a gentle reminder: *"A quick 10-minute walk now will reduce your post-meal blood sugar spike by up to 30%!"*
- **Targeted Home Workout Routines**:
  - Quick 15-minute bodyweight routines for PCOS, diabetes management, or senior mobility.
- **Taraweeh & Post-Iftar Cardio Optimizer**:
  - Calculates physical activity credits for Taraweeh prayers and suggests optimal timing for resistance training during Ramadan.

## 🩸 6. Women’s Hormonal & PCOS Nutrition
*Effort: Medium | Impact: High (Huge Market Need in South Asia)*

### What it does:
Specialized nutrition and symptom tracking for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), cycle syncing, and pregnancy.

### Key Capabilities:
- **Cycle-Synced Nutrition**:
  - Adapts macro targets and micronutrient focus across the 4 cycle phases (Follicular, Ovulatory, Luteal, Menstrual).
- **PCOS Anti-Inflammatory Guide**:
  - Prioritizes inositol-rich foods, low-glycemic carbs, and anti-inflammatory spices (turmeric, cinnamon, fenugreek/methi seeds).
- **Trimester-Specific Pregnancy Tracker**:
  - Tracks pregnancy weight progression, iron/folate intake, and gestational diabetes screening reminders.

## 🧑‍⚕️ 7. Doctor / Clinician Export Portal
*Effort: Low-Medium | Impact: High (Clinical Trust)*

### What it does:
Enables users to share verifiable metabolic and nutrition progress with their physician, endocrinologist, or dietitian.

### Key Capabilities:
- **1-Click Doctor PDF Summary**:
  - Formatted clinical report summarizing 30/90-day calorie adherence, macro ratios, blood sugar trends, and flagged risk events.
- **Secure Web Share Link**:
  - Time-limited, encrypted web link that a doctor can view on their clinic laptop during appointments.
- **Certified Nutritionist Directory**:
  - Connect with licensed Pakistani clinical dietitians (PNDS-certified) and PMDC-registered doctors for 1-on-1 consultations.
