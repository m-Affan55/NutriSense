--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Slide Title]: The Problem: South Asian Chronic Health Crisis & Nutrition Blindspot
[Visual Suggestion]: Split graphics showing regional Diabetes/Hypertension stats vs.
                    Western nutrition apps failing on local food.
[Key Bullet Points on Slide]:
  1. High Regional Disease Burden: South Asians face disproportionately high rates of
     Type-2 Diabetes, Hypertension, and early Cardiovascular Disease (CVD).
  2. Composite Cooking Blindspot: Traditional dishes (composite curries, ghee, tarka,
     deep-frying) cannot be accurately tracked by Western calorie databases.
  3. Generic AI Hallucinations & Risks: Standard AI chatbots lack clinical constraints
     and can recommend hazardous workouts (Valsalva lifts for hypertensive users) or
     fail during acute hypoglycemic episodes.
  4. Single-Device Household Reality: In many South Asian households, one smartphone
     is shared to manage the health of elderly diabetic parents, children, and spouses.

                        HACKATHON JUDGES Q&A PREPARATION
================================================================================
Q1: "How accurate is your food scanner on complex composite gravies?"
A1: "We use Google Gemini Vision calibrated specifically for South Asian culinary
     composition, combined with dynamic portion modifiers that allow users to
     correct portions in 1 tap, plus OpenFoodFacts barcode scanning for packaged items."

Q2: "What if the AI hallucinates dangerous medical or dietary advice?"
A2: "We do not let the AI output directly to the user. All recommendations pass through
     a 3-step RAG pipeline where an independent clinical risk evaluator and deterministic
     heuristic scanners inspect the output, automatically attaching warning blocks or
     blocking hazardous recommendations."

Q3: "How does this work for low-income or less tech-savvy users?"
A3: "We built NutriSense to be backward compatible down to Android 5.0 phones, engineered
     offline SQLite sync for poor internet areas, and integrated Neural Urdu Voice TTS
     with native Nastaleeq script for users who cannot read English."
