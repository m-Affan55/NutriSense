SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM (0:30 – 1:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: The South Asian Chronic Health & Nutrition Gap]

[Speaker]:
"Let’s look at why this problem is so urgent:

First, South Asians face a disproportionately high burden of Type-2 Diabetes and
Cardiovascular Disease—often diagnosed an entire decade earlier than global averages.

Second, our cooking methods are composite. Traditional dishes like Karahi, Nihari,
and Biryani have hidden cooking fats and gravies that standard databases cannot parse.

Third, generic AI advice is dangerous. Asking an unconstrained chatbot for a workout
can prescribe heavy breath-holding lifts that cause severe hypertensive spikes, or
miss acute hypoglycemia symptoms.

Lastly, in our region, a single smartphone is often shared to manage health for
elderly parents, children, and spouses. Single-user apps simply don't fit our families."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: NutriSense: Precision Health Built for Reality — 3 Core Pillars]

SLIDE 4: MULTIMODAL PLATE SCANNER (1:30 – 2:00)

[On Screen: Multimodal Plate Scanning & Dynamic Portions]

SLIDE 5: CLINICAL SAFETY & HYPOGLYCEMIA GUARD (2:00 – 2:30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: 3-Step Clinical RAG & Hypoglycemia Interceptor]

[Speaker]:
"We take clinical safety extremely seriously. We never allow raw AI responses to reach
the user directly.

Instead, we use a 3-step RAG pipeline: We retrieve patient history, generate
contextual guidance, and then pass it through an independent clinical risk evaluator
that attaches explicit medical warnings.

Crucially, we built a deterministic, sub-50-millisecond Hypoglycemia Scanner. If a user
logs blood sugar below 70 mg/dL or reports dizziness, the app immediately halts chat
and provides emergency fast-acting glucose protocols with an integrated GPS hospital locator."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 6: RAMADAN FASTING & CLINICAL WORKOUT ENGINE (2:30 – 3:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: Ramadan Fasting Suite & Clinical Workouts]

[Speaker]:
"During Ramadan, user metabolic patterns change drastically. NutriSense automatically
transforms into a dedicated Ramadan suite:
- Meal slots re-map dynamically to Sehri, Iftar, Post-Iftar, and Taraweeh Snack.
- Dual live countdown clocks track local Sehri and Iftar times.
- Daily hydration is intelligently paced across non-fasting night hours.

Alongside this, our Workout Engine creates condition-aware exercise routines:
- For Diabetics, it prioritizes post-meal glucose-blunting cardio.
- For Hypertensive users, it strictly prohibits heavy Valsalva maneuvers.
- For Arthritis, it substitutes high-impact jumps with zero-impact isometric routines."
