SLIDE 3: THE PROBLEM (1:00 – 1:30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: The South Asian Chronic Health & Nutrition Gap]

[Speaker]:
"Let’s look at why this is an urgent regional crisis:

First, South Asians face disproportionately high rates of Type-2 Diabetes and
Cardiovascular Disease—diagnosed up to a decade earlier than Western averages.

Second, our composite cooking (curries, ghee, tarka) cannot be parsed by Western
isolated-food databases.

Third, generic AI advice is dangerous. Asking an unconstrained chatbot for exercise
can prescribe heavy breath-holding lifts that cause severe hypertensive spikes, or
miss acute low blood sugar symptoms.

Lastly, in our region, a single smartphone is often shared to manage health for
elderly parents, children, and spouses."

SLIDE 6: CLINICAL SAFETY & HYPOGLYCEMIA GUARD (2:30 – 3:00)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: 3-Step Clinical RAG & Hypoglycemia Interceptor]

[Speaker]:
"We take clinical safety extremely seriously. We never allow raw AI responses to reach
the user directly.

Our 3-step RAG pipeline retrieves patient history, generates contextual guidance,
and validates output through an independent clinical risk evaluator.

Crucially, we engineered a deterministic, sub-50ms Hypoglycemia Scanner. If a user
logs blood sugar below 70 mg/dL or reports distress, the app immediately halts chat
and displays emergency glucose protocols with an integrated GPS hospital locator."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[On Screen: Ramadan Fasting Suite & Clinical Workouts]

[Speaker]:
"During Ramadan, NutriSense automatically transforms into a specialized suite:
- Meal slots re-map to Sehri, Iftar, Post-Iftar, and Taraweeh Snack.

Alongside this, our Workout Engine creates condition-aware exercise routines:
- For Diabetics: Post-meal glucose-blunting cardio and insulin sensitization.
- For Hypertensive users: Strictly prohibits heavy Valsalva maneuvers.
- For Arthritis: Replaces high-impact movements with zero-impact isometric routines."
