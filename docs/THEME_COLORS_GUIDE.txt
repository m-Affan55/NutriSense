================================================================================
                    NUTRISENSE - PROJECT THEME COLOR SPECIFICATION
                           (GENERAL GREEN DARK THEME)
================================================================================

This document outlines the complete design system color palette, hex codes, 
RGB values, opacity rules, and UI component usage for the NutriSense general theme.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIMARY BRAND & ACCENT COLORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Primary Brand Green (Neon Health Green)
  - HEX Code:       #00E676
  - RGB:            rgb(0, 230, 118)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF00E676)
  - Usage & Role:   Primary brand identity, active navigation capsules, primary 
                    action buttons ("Log Meal with AI", "Confirm & Log"), protein 
                    macro badges, success notifications, and brand icons.
  - Psychology:     Evokes vitality, clean health, organic nutrition, and active energy.

• Secondary Accent (Warm Warning / Focus Orange)
  - HEX Code:       #FF6D00
  - RGB:            rgb(255, 109, 0)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFFFF6D00)
  - Usage & Role:   Secondary alerts, warning banners, cautionary dietary advice, 
                    and energy-related highlights.

• Teal / Mint Gradient Blend
  - HEX Code:       #00BCD4
  - RGB:            rgb(0, 188, 212)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF00BCD4)
  - Usage & Role:   Paired with #00E676 in linear gradients for hero banners, 
                    hydration badges, and active progress rings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BACKGROUND & SURFACE HIERARCHY (DARK MODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NutriSense utilizes a multi-tiered dark surface architecture to create depth 
and reduce eye strain in low-light environments.

• Tier 1: Deep Void Background (Scaffold & Base Canvas)
  - HEX Code:       #0D0F14
  - RGB:            rgb(13, 15, 20)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF0D0F14)
  - Usage & Role:   App scaffold background, full-screen canvas, and bottom sheet base.

• Tier 2: Surface Dark (Cards & Dashboard Tiles)
  - HEX Code:       #161A22
  - RGB:            rgb(22, 26, 34)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF161A22)
  - Usage & Role:   Main dashboard cards, meal timeline tiles, activity summaries, 
                    form text field inputs, and navigation bar background.

• Tier 3: Elevated Surface (Modals, Popups & Floating Dialogs)
  - HEX Code:       #1E232E
  - RGB:            rgb(30, 35, 46)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF1E232E)
  - Usage & Role:   AlertDialogs, popup menus, dropdown selection menus, and 
                    interactive chip backgrounds.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TYPOGRAPHY & TEXT CONTRAST PALETTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Text Primary (Crisp High-Contrast White)
  - HEX Code:       #FFFFFF (100% Opacity)
  - RGB:            rgb(255, 255, 255)
  - Flutter Code:   Colors.white / Color(0xFFFFFFFF)
  - Usage & Role:   Headline titles, screen titles, active button text, vital metrics, 
                    and primary numbers (Calories, Protein grams).

• Text Secondary (Cool Slate Grey)
  - HEX Code:       #8A94A6
  - RGB:            rgb(138, 148, 166)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF8A94A6) / Colors.white70
  - Usage & Role:   Subtitle labels, unselected navigation icons/labels, body 
                    descriptions, date stamps, and helper hints.

• Text Muted / Hint (Subtle Grey)
  - HEX Code:       #555E6D (or White with 38% Opacity)
  - RGB:            rgb(85, 94, 109)
  - Flutter Code:   Colors.white38 / Color(0xFF555E6D)
  - Usage & Role:   Input placeholder hints, timestamp footnotes, and disabled states.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FUNCTIONAL NUTRITION & STATUS COLOR CODING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

NutriSense assigns standardized clinical color codes to nutrients and statuses:

• Calories (Energy Gold)
  - HEX Code:       #FFD166
  - RGB:            rgb(255, 209, 102)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFFFFD166)
  - Indicator:      🔥 Flame icon, total daily calorie targets, calorie progress ring.

• Protein (Muscle & Health Green)
  - HEX Code:       #00E676
  - RGB:            rgb(0, 230, 118)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF00E676)
  - Indicator:      💪 Muscle icon, protein progress bars, high-protein meal tags.

• Carbohydrates (Energy Cyan)
  - HEX Code:       #00BCD4 / #448AFF
  - RGB:            rgb(0, 188, 212)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF00BCD4)
  - Indicator:      🌾 Grain icon, carbohydrate tracking pill.

• Healthy Fats (Metabolic Coral / Red-Orange)
  - HEX Code:       #FF6B6B / #FF5252
  - RGB:            rgb(255, 107, 107)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFFFF6B6B)
  - Indicator:      🥑 Avocado icon, dietary fat tracking pill.

• Hydration (Water Drop Blue)
  - HEX Code:       #00BCD4
  - RGB:            rgb(0, 188, 212)
  - Flutter Code:   Color(0xFF00BCD4)
  - Indicator:      💧 Water drop icon, +250ml / +500ml quick logging buttons.

• Emergency, Critical Alerts & Delete Actions
  - HEX Code:       #E53935 / #FF3B30
  - RGB:            rgb(229, 57, 53)
  - Flutter Code:   Colors.red / Color(0xFFE53935)
  - Indicator:      🏥 Emergency Hospital Finder icon, Delete Account button, 
                    critical allergen warnings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GLASSMORPHISM, BORDERS & TRANSLUCENCY RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• Frosted Glass Bottom Navigation Bar:
  - Background:     Color(0xFF161A22) with 70% opacity (withAlpha: 180)
  - Blur Effect:    BackdropFilter(sigmaX: 15, sigmaY: 15)
  - Border:         Border.all(color: Colors.white.withAlpha(20), width: 1.5)

• Active Nav Capsule:
  - Background:     Color(0xFF00E676) with 12% opacity (withAlpha: 30)
  - Icon & Text:    Color(0xFF00E676)

• Subtle Card Borders:
  - Color:          Colors.white with 8% opacity (Colors.white.withAlpha(20))
  - Radius:         16px to 24px rounded corners

• Neon Glow Shadow (Primary CTA):
  - Shadow Color:   Color(0xFF00E676) with 30% opacity (withAlpha: 80)
  - Blur Radius:    16px
  - Offset:         Offset(0, 4)
