================================================================================
                    NUTRISENSE - COMPREHENSIVE USER GUIDE
================================================================================
Welcome to the NutriSense User Guide. This manual contains detailed step-by-step
operating instructions, feature breakdowns, and screenshot guidelines to help
you navigate and leverage the app's localized nutritional AI, fasting mode,
family profile tracking, clinical workout plans, and predictive coaching.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. GETTING STARTED & ACCOUNT SETUP
----------------------------------
* Installation Instructions:
  - Android Devices: Locate and download the NutriSense APK (v1.0.0) from the 
    official GitHub repository releases tab. Tap the downloaded file and follow 
    the system prompt to install (allow installation from unknown sources).
  - Web Platforms: Open any modern web browser on your computer or phone and 
    navigate to the NutriSense Web Application URL hosted on Vercel.
  - Note: NutriSense is currently optimized for Android and Web and is not 
    deployed for iOS systems.

* Account Sign Up & Sign In:
  - Launch the application to open the Welcome Screen.
  - Tap "Sign Up" to create a new profile. Enter a valid email address and a 
    strong password. Confirm your password and tap submit.
  - Check your inbox for a confirmation email to verify your registration.
  - If you already have an account, enter your email and password on the Login 
    Screen and tap "Sign In".
  - If you forget your password, tap "Forgot Password?" to receive a secure 
    password reset link via email.

* Interactive Onboarding Flow:
  - On your first login, the app launches an interactive Onboarding dialog.
  - Step 1: Input your age, gender, height (cm), and weight (kg).
  - Step 2: Set your physical activity multiplier (Sedentary, Lightly Active, 
    Moderately Active, Very Active).
  - Step 3: Choose your primary fitness goal (Fat Loss, Muscle Gain, Healthy 
    Lifestyle, or Maintenance).
  - Step 4: Select any existing medical conditions (Diabetes, Hypertension, 
    PCOS, High Cholesterol, or None).
  - Step 5: Specify dietary restrictions/allergies (Halal, Vegetarian, Vegan, 
    Nut Allergy, Gluten-Free, Lactose Intolerant, or None).
  - Tap "Calculate Targets". The system uses Google Gemini to compute your 
    daily calorie goal and water budget, loading them into your profile.

2. EXPLORING THE MAIN DASHBOARD
-------------------------------
* Dynamic Header:
  - At the top of the dashboard, you are greeted by a personalized banner 
    (e.g., "Hello, Affan!") alongside a dynamic calendar date switcher.

* Calorie & Nutrition Ring:
  - A prominent circular ring displays your calorie progress. It visualizes 
    "Calories Logged" vs. your prescribed "Daily Caloric Budget".
  - The center of the ring dynamically displays the remaining calories left 
    for the current day.

* Macronutrient Breakdown:
  - Directly below the calorie ring, horizontal progress bars track the 
    breakdown of Protein, Carbohydrates, and Fats consumed (in grams) against 
    your daily macro targets.

* Google Health Connect Integration:
  - If enabled, the dashboard shows real-time physical telemetry synced from 
    Google Health Connect: Steps taken, Active Calories burned, and Distance 
    traveled.

* Today's Meals Timeline:
  - Shows a chronological list of meals logged today.
  - Each item displays the food description, calculated calories, macro badge, 
    and quick actions to Edit values or Delete the entry.

* Hydration Tracker:
  - Allows logging water intake with 1-tap logging chips (+250ml cup, +500ml 
    bottle) or entering a custom amount.
  - A circular blue ring tracks your hydration progress against your target.

* Habit Score Card:
  - Displays a rolling habit consistency rating out of 100 with active streak indicators to reward consecutive logging days.

3. LOGGING MEALS WITH AI (ZERO-EFFORT NUTRITION)
------------------------------------------------
* Step-by-Step AI Meal Logging:
  - Step 1: Open the app and tap the "Meals" tab in the bottom navigation bar 
    (or tap the "+ Add Meal" shortcut button on the main dashboard).
  - Step 2: In the natural language text field, describe what you ate. You can 
    use English, Urdu, or mixed Roman-Urdu phrases (e.g., "1 plate Chicken 
    Biryani and half glass Raita" or "2 Aloo Parathas with 1 cup Chai").
  - Step 3: Select the Meal Category (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack).
  - Step 4: (Optional) If you logged a common regional meal, tap any of the 
    quick suggestion chips (e.g., "Egg & Roti", "Daal Chawal") to instantly 
    pre-fill the field.
  - Step 5: Tap "Log Meal with AI". The system queries Gemini, estimates 
    nutritional metrics based on South Asian cuisine data, and displays the 
    calculated macronutrients in a preview card.
  - Step 6: Verify the details and tap "Confirm and Log". The meal is saved.

* Editing and Correcting AI Values:
  - If you need to override the AI's estimation, tap the "Edit Values" button 
    on the preview card.
  - Modify the calories, protein, carbs, or fats manually using the numerical 
    input fields, then tap "Save Custom Log".

4. INTERACTING WITH THE AI HEALTH COACH & VOICE ASSISTANT
---------------------------------------------------------
* AI Dietitian Chat:
  - Tap the "Coach" tab to start a conversation with your AI Dietitian Coach.
  - You can type questions in English or Urdu (e.g., "Explain how to manage 
    hypertension with my diet" or "Suggest a high-protein dinner recipe").
  - The AI dietitian processes your query against your profile metrics and 
    medical conditions to return clinically-safe, bulleted markdown guides.

* Text-to-Speech Language Playback:
  - On any message returned by the AI Coach, tap the green 🔊 Speaker icon.
  - The voice assistant will read the text aloud. The audio synthesizer 
    dynamically switch pronunciations to read Urdu text in Urdu accent and 
    English text in English accent.

* Healthcare Clinic Quick Finder:
  - In the AI Coach header, locate the red 🏥 Hospital icon.
  - Tapping this icon instantly redirects you to the geospatial emergency 
    clinic directory for fast access during health emergencies.

5. RAMADAN FASTING MODE
-----------------------
* Activating Ramadan Mode:
  - Go to Settings or look at the top of the main dashboard.
  - Locate the "Ramadan Fasting Mode" toggle and switch it ON. The dashboard 
    will immediately update with crescent moon motifs, Islamic pattern borders, 
    and specialized widgets.

* Sehri & Iftar Timings:
  - The dashboard displays a live countdown timer showing the hours, minutes, 
    and seconds remaining until the next meal window (e.g., "Time until Iftar: 
    3h 15m 12s" or "Time until Sehri: 5h 20m 00s").
  - Timings are calculated automatically based on your GPS coordinates.

* Ramadan Meal Logging:
  - The standard Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner slots are replaced by Sehri, 
    Iftar, and Post-Taraweeh Snack meal categories.

* Ramadan Paced Hydration:
  - Because hydration is restricted to non-fasting hours, the water tracker 
    presents quick-log buttons aligned with fasting schedules:
    - "+500ml Iftar" (Fast Break Hydration)
    - "+250ml Taraweeh" (During Prayer Hydration)
    - "+500ml Sehri" (Pre-Fast Hydration)

6. MANAGING FAMILY & DEPENDENT PROFILES
---------------------------------------
* Setting Up Dependent Profiles:
  - Open the navigation menu and go to Settings -> Family Profiles.
  - Tap "+ Add Family Member".
  - Enter the family member's Name, select their Relationship (Child, Parent, 
    Spouse, Sibling), and input their age, gender, height, and weight.
  - Select their specific medical conditions and dietary restrictions.
  - Select a custom avatar color to represent them, then tap "Save Profile".

* Logging Meals for Dependents:
  - When logging a meal, tap the profile dropdown menu (which defaults to 
    "Myself") and select your family member's name.
  - Input their meal description and tap "Log Meal with AI". The macros will 
    be calculated based on their health profile and saved under their record.

* Viewing Family Dashboard Telemetry:
  - Open the profile selector at the top-left of the main dashboard.
  - Tap the avatar of your child or parent.
  - The main dashboard, calorie rings, step tracker, and timelines will 
    instantly transition to display that specific family member's data.

7. EMERGENCY CLINIC & HOSPITAL FINDER
-------------------------------------
* Geolocation Emergency Querying:
  - Go to Settings -> Nearby Clinics & Hospitals (or tap the Hospital icon 
    in the Coach tab).
  - Grant the app GPS location permission. The app queries the OpenStreetMap 
    Overpass API to scan for medical centers.
  - View the list of hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies sorted by proximity 
    within a 5km to 15km radius.

* Navigating to Facilities:
  - Review the distance (in kilometers), phone contact link, and 24/7 
    emergency indicators on each hospital card.
  - Tap "Get Directions" on your chosen facility. The app instantly opens 
    Google Maps with pre-populated turn-by-turn routing to guide you safely.

8. GROCERY LIST & WEEKLY HEALTH REPORT
--------------------------------------
* AI Smart Grocery List:
  - Open Settings and tap "Smart Grocery List".
  - The app aggregates your logged meals from the past 7 days, evaluates your 
    macronutrient gaps, and compiles a categorized grocery list.
  - The list recommends items in groups (Vitamins, Proteins, Grains) and 
    flags items needed for your recommended healthy food swaps (e.g., listing 
    whole-wheat flour to swap out white flour).

* Exporting Weekly PDF Summaries:
  - Open Settings and tap "Export Weekly Summary (PDF)".
  - The app compiles your 7-day health metrics (average calorie intake, water 
    totals, steps, active alerts, and AI dietitian comments) into a professional 
    PDF document.
  - Tap "Save PDF" to download it locally, print it, or share it with your doctor.

9. APP SETTINGS, LANGUAGE & ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
----------------------------------------------
* Language Toggle:
  - Open Settings and locate the Language section.
  - Tap "Urdu" to instantly translate all menus, labels, and text fields into 
    Urdu. Tap "English" to switch back.

* System Theme Settings:
  - Under Settings, locate the Theme selector.
  - Toggle between Light Mode and Dark Mode. Dark Mode uses high-contrast dark 
    backgrounds for optimal reading comfort and battery optimization.

* Account Settings & Security:
  - Change Password: Tap "Change Password", enter your current password, type 
    your new password, and confirm to save.
  - Account Deletion: Tap "Delete Account". Read the warning detailing that all 
    records will be permanently removed. Enter your password and tap "Confirm 
    Deletion" to purge all Supabase rows.

10. PREDICTIVE AI COACHING & NUTRITIONAL SWAPS
----------------------------------------------
* Accessing Predictive Coaching:
  - Tap the "Coaching" tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  - This screen displays your algorithmic 7-day habit score and healthy food 
    swap recommendations.

* Habit Score Analysis:
  - View your large consistency ring showing your score out of 100. A score of 
    80+ indicates excellent compliance.
  - Review the line chart illustrating your habit consistency over the past 
    7 days to track your progress.

* Recommended Food Swaps:
  - The screen highlights specific food swaps compiled by Gemini based on your 
    logged meals (e.g., recommending brown rice instead of white rice).
  - Each food swap card lists the suggested healthier ingredient alongside a 
    clinical explanation detailing how the swap improves your blood sugar, cholesterol, or blood pressure.

11. AI-GENERATED CLINICAL WORKOUT PLANS
---------------------------------------
* Viewing Daily Workouts:
  - Tap the "Workout" tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  - Tap "Generate Workout Plan" to have Gemini create a custom schedule based 
    on your onboarding metrics and medical restrictions.
  - Use the day-of-week tab bar selector at the top (Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.) to 
    view the routine planned for each day.

* Workout Walkthrough & Execution:
  - View the overall focus of the day (e.g., "Strength", "Flexibility", "Rest").
  - Tap the Warm-Up list to review warm-up routines.
  - Swipe through the active exercise cards. Each card displays the exercise name, 
    number of sets, target repetitions, rest interval, execution form tips, 
    and clinical safety flags.
  - As you complete each exercise, tap the circular checkbox to check it off. 
    The progress bar at the top of the screen will update in real-time.
  - Tap the Cool-Down list at the bottom to complete your cooldown stretch.

* Regenerating Plans:
  - If you switch to Ramadan Mode or update your medical profile, tap "Regenerate 
    Workout" to recalculate your weekly training schedule to match your new state.
