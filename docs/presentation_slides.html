The Challenge

The South Asian Chronic Health & Nutrition Gap

Severe Metabolic Burden
South Asians face disproportionately high rates of Type-2 Diabetes, Hypertension, and early CVD, often diagnosed a decade earlier than Western counterparts.
Composite Cooking Blindspot
Western apps assume isolated ingredients (chicken breast + rice). Traditional dishes (Biryani, Karahi, Nihari, Daal Tarka) have hidden oils and composite gravies that Western databases cannot parse.
Hazardous Generic AI Advice
Generic ChatGPT prompts lack clinical guardrails—prescribing heavy Valsalva maneuvers to hypertensive patients or failing during acute low blood sugar emergencies.
Single-Device Family Reality
In most households, one smartphone is used to manage health for elderly diabetic parents, growing children, and spouses. Single-user apps fail this dynamic.
